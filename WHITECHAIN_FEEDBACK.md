CreatorAdvance targets Whitechain Sepolia's Tokenize track. We verified the public RPC chain ID (`1874`), organizer USDT contract bytecode, symbol, and six-decimal precision. The chain and token configuration was straightforward to confirm from the event page and network RPC.

The main remaining friction is completing the exact test-USDT acquisition path: the prize page links YetiTrade testnet, but we have not yet completed a funding or settlement transaction. A concise official walkthrough that starts with a Sepolia address and ends with a confirmed balance of the organizer's exact USDT contract would reduce uncertainty. We also have not yet verified source through the Whitechain explorer because the app contract is awaiting deployment approval.

The prize criteria clearly distinguish the organizer's USDT from a self-minted demo token and require an explorer-verifiable payment. That is useful and keeps the demo honest. We will update this note after the live transaction and explorer verification.
